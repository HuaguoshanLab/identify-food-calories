## Decisions Made

- Vector rows are unique by `publication_id`, `name_id` and `vector_space_id`; the active pointer cannot make different model/dimension/adapter vectors comparable.
- Relation revocation is a new audited `revoked` record, never a destructive update of the earlier controlled assertion.
- Activation approvals carry release, dataset, code, retrieval and embedding hashes, while switching the singleton pointer remains a separate transaction.
