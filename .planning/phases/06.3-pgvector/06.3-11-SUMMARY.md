## Decisions Made

- The worker is scheduled through `asyncio.to_thread` because its durable job transaction is synchronous; the scheduler does not become a second job authority.
- Unexpected loop errors are isolated so an embedding outage cannot terminate the FastAPI process or exact/text fallback.
